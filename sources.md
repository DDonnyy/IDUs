# Source register

Source IDs identify provenance and limits, not universal authority. The public toolkit retains
transferable guidance and synthetic fix/keep examples. Private manuscript choices, full feedback
and historical comparisons are not part of installed skills. Do not infer scientific truth or AI
authorship from these editorial observations.

pp. 7–8 rhetorical moves; pp. 11–14 tense, gap and aims; pp. 15–16 paragraph order. Supports

## S-METHOD

## S-CUS-R1R3

Original journal feedback from one manuscript: structure, definitions, claims, figures and
methodological support. Supports scoped completeness/evidence checks, particularly EC5–EC10.
A request for clarification or validation is not proof of an error or a requirement for every paper.

## S-CUS-R2

Another review of that submission: rationale for thresholds/metrics, anomalous values and reporting
completeness. A request to inspect a value is not evidence that it is wrong. Author/journal-specific
formatting requirements remain local.

## S-TEACHER

A documented comparison of author-supplied text with language-expert edits. Supports the A–G
language checks, wording candidates and contextual punctuation advice. Distinguish an explicit
comment from an inferred reason; accepted edits can contain slips or change scientific meaning.
The complete comparison and individual decisions stay in the private source archive.
Further comparisons inform positive academic calibration: direct subject/action relations,
explicit parallels and named quantities. Reasons inferred from edits are analyst interpretations.
Public examples are synthetic; useful readability improvements are distinguished from mandatory
grammar repairs and private house style.

## S-AUDITS

Historical agent analyses of those materials. They supply failure/keep examples and safeguards
against lost hedges, added exclusivity, reversed comparisons and unsupported aggregate claims.
These are interpretations to assess, not original reviewer verdicts or model-performance evidence.

## S-PROJECT

Source/version mapping and scoped author settings. They establish which text/profile a past review
concerned, not defaults for new users. Historical profiles are kept privately when available.

## Rules derived by synthesis

The distinction between wording, evidence and application status, meaning safeguards and the
compact drafting workflow synthesise these sources and editorial verification. No empirical
claim of comprehensive error detection is made. Original documents and detailed locators may be
available locally under `references/local/`; a clone/installed skill does not include them.
