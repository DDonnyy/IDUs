# Governance

Maintainers assess scoped contributions and record accept, request changes, duplicate, local-only
or decline with reasons. Prefer a narrow existing-ID clarification/example over duplication.
Expertise and popularity do not establish universal language rules or scientific truth.

Merging a proposal JSON alone does not activate it. Acceptance updates canonical guidance, related
resources and fix/keep calibration, then generated skills and a versioned release. CI checks
software/package behaviour, not editorial validity. Discuss evidence/context respectfully and
revisit decisions when new evidence or failed keep cases warrant it.

Use focused PRs to `main`; retain Apache-2.0/source rights and share only authorised material.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
