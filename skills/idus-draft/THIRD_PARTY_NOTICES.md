# Sources and third-party material

The Apache-2.0 licence covers original IDUs toolkit contributions, including original code,
prompts and editorial guidance. It does not grant rights to external research papers, lecture
slides, reviewer reports, teacher-edited manuscripts or third-party quotations.

[The source register](https://github.com/DDonnyy/IDUs/blob/main/sources.md) records the toolkit's evidence and adaptations. Examples taken from
source material serve editorial analysis; they are not proof of universal language rules or
scientific validity. Keep attribution, source scope, limitations and rejected/keep examples.
Do not replace the source register with a general copyright claim.

Original PDFs and confidential reference material belong under `references/local/`, which is
ignored and excluded from every distribution. Do not attach them to public issues or PRs.
Use synthetic examples or excerpts the contributor has permission to share. A contribution's
sharing declaration must be reviewed; passing validation cannot establish publication rights.

Projects studied for packaging patterns are linked in
[the repository design notes](https://github.com/DDonnyy/IDUs/blob/main/docs/repository-maintenance.md). No third-party implementation is
vendored merely because its repository is discussed. Retain the applicable notice/licence if
third-party code or assets are added later.
