# Optional first-draft brief

Free-form notes are enough; leave unknown details blank. Do not fill gaps with guesses.

- Requested scope: whole first version / specified sections / reshape a rough version.
- Paper type, audience, venue and approximate length, if known:
- Research question and intended contribution:
- Author's intended argument and emphasis:
- Supplied material: notes, rough text, tables, methods, reference excerpts (paths or pasted text).
- What was done and found, with numerical conditions/units:
- What is planned, uncertain, contradictory or not yet analysed:
- Supplied literature findings/citation keys and what has actually been checked:
- Definitions, frozen terms, spelling and relevant project settings:
- Preferred output: chat or a new file in the selected workspace.

Use idus-draft. Do not require a complete brief before drafting supported sections.
Source labels may be simple filenames/headings or N1/N2.
